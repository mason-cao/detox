How the Isle works

Each app you use is a resident of your isle. The ones you spend the most time with get the biggest plots.

Sunlight (☀) grows while you stay under your daily budget. Starshards (✦) come from streaks and milestones — they're the rarer currency.

The sky and sun/moon track your local time. Close the app, touch grass, come back tomorrow.

Shortcuts: 1–8 switch tabs, D toggles theme, ? opens this panel, Esc closes it.
