Where do the sprites come from?

SVG world is the canvas — it still needs art. With ~50 tracked apps each becoming a resident, you can't draw a unique sprite per app. The system is: ~8 archetypes (scribe / builder / jester / banished / farmer / musician / wanderer / sheriff) and apps inherit one based on category. Plus 8 unique buildings.

A

Programmatic SVG (DIY)

Each sprite is ~30 lines of inline SVG <rect>s. Walk cycles via animating limb rects. Color variations per app via CSS variables.

Effort: ~3 days for 8 archetypes + walk cycles + 8 buildings.
Look: Unique to your project, geometric, slightly abstract. The rects above are the actual fidelity.
Pros: No external assets, no licensing, ships in the repo, every pixel under your control.
Cons: Won't look quite as polished as hand-drawn. The "indie pixel art on a budget" aesthetic.

B

Free CC0 sprite pack recommended

Drop in a curated pack — kenney.nl "Tiny Town", OpenGameArt isometric sets, or itch.io CC0 packs. Real hand-drawn pixel art with walk cycles already done.

Effort: ~1 day to integrate (pick pack, slice atlas, map to archetypes).
Look: Genuine Stardew-tier polish.
Pros: Best quality-per-effort. CC0 means no attribution needed (we can credit anyway). Walk cycles done.
Cons: Slight "I've seen this in another game" feel if the pack is popular. Mitigated by mixing two packs + custom palette tinting.

C

Emoji with heavy CSS treatment

Keep current emoji approach but apply filter: contrast saturate drop-shadow, outline, walk-bob animation. The samples above are the actual treatment.

Effort: ~half day.
Look: Always recognizable as emoji-with-makeup. Apple/Google emoji art conflicts with pixel aesthetic.
Honest take: Cheap, fast, ceiling is low. Listed for completeness.

D

Commission custom sprites

Hire a pixel artist to draw 8 archetypes + 8 buildings + walk cycles to your spec.

Effort: 1–3 weeks turnaround, async with the artist.
Look: Top tier. Yours alone.
Cost: Real money + scheduling. Best path eventually, premature now.
Honest take: If you ship the redesign with B and people like it, upgrade to D for v2.

Recommended hybrid: B for residents (walk cycles are the hard part — buy them) and A for buildings (geometric, easy to author programmatically, gives the project its own architectural identity). Pick a single letter, or say "B+A hybrid" / "all B" / etc.
