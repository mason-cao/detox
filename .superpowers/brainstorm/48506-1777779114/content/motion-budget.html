How much is happening on screen at any moment?

"Less static" is a motion problem. The lever is ambient activity — what's moving when you're not interacting. Three tiers, each tied to whether the world reacts to live tracking data.

residents bob in place · clouds drift
A

Light — ambient bob

Each resident stands at their building, gentle vertical bob (2 px, 2s loop). Clouds drift, sun arcs. No path movement.

Effort: Easy. Pure CSS animations.
Live data binding: None — the world doesn't know what's running right now.
Feels like: A pretty diorama. Better than static, still passive.
B

Medium — walks + live binding recommended

Residents wander slow paths between buildings (1 step / 4 s, 4-frame walk). The frontmost app resident has a soft glow aura and is doing a "working" animation at their building. Background residents bob. Smoke from chimneys. Doors open when you click a building.

Effort: Real lift. Path system + activity poller (every 10 s) + walk-cycle frames. ~1 week.
Live data binding: Yes. The Isle reflects what you're actively using right now, not just historical totals. Spec §3.1 says residents change behavior; this delivers it.
Feels like: A living town that watches you back. The product's whole point.
schedules · particles · sound
C

Heavy — full schedules + particles + audio

Residents follow a daily schedule (wake 7 am, work, lunch break, evening stroll). Particle systems (smoke, snow, fireflies). Optional chiptune ambience. Sheriff sprite walks out for blocks.

Effort: 3+ weeks. Ongoing tuning.
Risk: Distracting from the actual screen-time data. Performance on slower Macs.
Honest take: Cool but premature. Ship B, see what users want next.

My strong recommendation: B. The "frontmost app glows" idea is the single highest-leverage feature — it makes the Isle a live mirror of your behavior, not a daily report card.
