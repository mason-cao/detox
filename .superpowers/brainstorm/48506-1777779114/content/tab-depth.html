How deep does the polish go on the 7 non-Isle tabs?

Registry, Chronicle, Charter, Rule Board, Postcards, Study, Market are functional but flat. "Full sweep" can mean three different ambitions. Using the Rule Board as a worked example.

⚖ THE RULE BOARD
YouTube — banished remove
Twitter — banished remove
Slack — daily ration 60m remove
A

Light — themed re-skin

Replace flat backgrounds with parchment / chalkboard textures, swap fonts, repaint buttons as wax seals. No new motion, no new interactions. Same layout.

Effort: ~2 days for all 7 tabs.
Feel: Pretty wallpaper. Functional underneath is unchanged.
⚖ T H E R U L E B O A R D
YouTube — banished by sheriff
Twitter — at sea
Slack — ration 60m
+ chalk-dust particles when adding a rule, sheriff sprite walks out, sound: chalk scratch
B

Medium — diegetic motion recommended

Same layout, but adding a rule plays a chalk-write animation, the rule strikes through with chalk, the sheriff sprite walks into the rule-board scene to "enforce", ration bars are pixel hourglasses that drain. Each tab gets one signature animation tied to its primary action.

Effort: ~5 days for all 7 tabs.
Per-tab signatures: Registry — parchment page-turn between filter views. Chronicle — quill draws the bar charts. Charter — wax seal stamps when goal saved. Rule Board — chalk write/erase. Postcards — stamp slams down. Study — candle flame flickers, matches dark mode. Market — coin-clink on purchase.
Feel: Every interaction is a tiny ceremony. The tabs feel built, not just styled.
[interior of town hall · sheriff at desk · clock ticks]
C

Heavy — full "rooms" with ambient sprites

Each sub-tab is a rendered interior scene with the relevant NPC at work. Click into Rule Board → camera pans inside Town Hall → sheriff is at his desk, you talk to him. Rules layout overlaid as a dialog.

Effort: 2.5+ weeks for all 7. Big art ask.
Risk: Friction on every interaction. "I just want to add a block" → wait for camera pan.
Honest take: Cool concept, wrong product. People use Detox to quickly set rules and move on.

Recommend B. Per-tab signature animation gives the "made with care" feel without making it harder to actually use the app. Pick A / B / C.
