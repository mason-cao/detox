A

Pure diegetic — buildings only

Click any building on the Isle to walk inside. From a sub-tab you return via a "back to Isle" door at top-left. Hover any building → name label + currency cost (if locked).

Pros: Maximum immersion. The Isle is the menu.
Cons: Sub-tabs feel "stranded" — you don't see what other rooms exist until you go back.

B

Buildings + persistent compass

Same diegetic nav, but a small compass card pins to top-right showing the 8 destinations. Highlights current room. From any sub-tab, you can jump directly to a sibling without bouncing through the Isle.

Pros: Game feel + you never feel lost. Mobile-friendly (compass collapses to a single icon).
Cons: One more chrome element — but it's small and earns its keep.
